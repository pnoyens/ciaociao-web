HOW IT WORKS

3 steps to building your pack

Intelligent discovery

Our AI-powered engine continuously monitors LinkedIn, identifying discussions, posts, and comments that align with your brand. We filter out the noise and deliver a curated feed of high-value opportunities where you can make an impact.

Context analysis

Not every conversation is worth joining. Our proprietary SocialTemp™ Score analyzes context, sentiment, and participant engagement to tell you how receptive a discussion is. We help you understand the dynamics before you engage.

Strategic engagement

With our insights, you can confidently join conversations at the perfect moment. Share valuable perspectives, contribute meaningfully, and build authentic relationships. We handle the discovery—you handle the human connection.

Frequently asked questions

Is this another aggressive sales automation tool?

No. CiaoCiao is fundamentally different. We focus on guidance, not automation. We help you find the right conversations and understand the context, but you control when and how to engage. The human element is what makes it work.

How do you access LinkedIn data?

We use the official LinkedIn API with OAuth authentication. We never ask for your password, and your account security is our top priority. We also supplement with publicly available data through intelligent search.

Is this only for large companies with established brands?

Not at all. CiaoCiao is perfect for building a brand from the ground up. Whether you're a solo founder or a Fortune 500 company, our platform helps you find your voice and your audience.

Can I use this for my organization's LinkedIn page?

Yes! In fact, that's exactly what we're designed for. All engagement happens through your official organization page, building brand authority and trust with your audience.
